# Product

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
Policy researchers and assessors inspect reported Indian state AI activity, then open the source behind a record. Readers use ordinary laptop and phone screens, often in daylight. Readable labels and an explicit research cutoff matter more than decoration.

## Principles
Keep the existing tracker. Improve legibility without replacing its layout. Use concrete counts and describe them as reported activity, not verified delivery or policy quality. Preserve source links and filters. Keep the distinction between report date and action date visible. Avoid promotional language, em dashes and unsupported accuracy claims.

## User direction
The tracker is the sole public dashboard. The observatory site is not a design reference. Compact technical writing and clear diagrams in the supplied Vahini references inform the report, not the policy facts. Keep numbers on one line and use strong table-header contrast.
