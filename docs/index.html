    What counts

    A record is one concrete step involving AI taken by a state government or a body that answers to it: a policy or draft, a new body or institution, a signed agreement, a deployment in a public service, a budget commitment, a training programme, or a rule. High Court directions are included and marked as judiciary.

    Central government actions in a named state are included and can be filtered with the Level control. Left out: opinion pieces, speeches with no concrete step, company news with no state body involved, crime stories with no policy response, and party politics.
