# Reference research appendix

This appendix preserves a separately assembled collection of 85 events, 113 sources and 137 event-source links across twelve states, dated 2024 through 10 November 2026. Its event dates, inclusion rules and evidence fields differ from the news tracker. It is not added to tracker totals.

Read RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md with data/reference/events.csv, sources.csv and source_observations.csv. The notes are inherited source research, not a new assertion that every source was re-opened for this release. The final check read the Rajasthan GPU RFP; the Odisha Gazette and Tamil Nadu mission-order links timed out. Source-level limitations remain part of the data.

Drafts, approvals, Gazette publication, tenders and launches are distinct actions. Amounts describing public budget provisions, private investment intentions and actual spending must not be combined. A government or partner host does not make a syndicated article an independent source.
